Accounting

Ceres Enterprises has developed a highly detailed, user-friendly accounting system to track hotel financial progress, enabling comparisons of individual line items via percentages or costs per occupied room. Profit and loss statements help General Managers compare performance against last year and budget while identifying areas needing improvement. We monitor several metrics, including top-line revenue, gross operating profit, overall labor expense, outstanding accounts receivable, market share results, and overall guest satisfaction.

Payroll

Our payroll system is driven at the property level and executed at Ceres Enterprises corporate headquarters where frequent audits take place, approvals are made, and checks and balances are done. This centralized approach ensures accuracy, compliance, and timely processing while maintaining the flexibility needed for property-level management.

Human Resources

Ceres Enterprises offers professional human resource assistance to our properties. Benefit offerings to associates are consistently enhanced through shopping for and obtaining the best healthcare benefits at the best rates. We offer supplemental insurance to all associates and consistently conduct wage surveys to ensure competitiveness. Human Resources drives updates on workers compensation, OSHA regulations, new hire documentation, annual reviews, coaching and counseling, associate handbook updates and codes of conduct.

Asset Management

Ceres Enterprises has developed unique and cost-effective programs for hotel renovations and construction. Each guest room is inspected and preventative maintenance is performed quarterly, including plumbing, HVAC, paint touch up and any additional items requiring repair. Capital requests are submitted by property leaders each year to the Director of Asset Management, ranked by importance, and submitted to the Director of Finance and President for approval.

Purchasing

Purchasing is an extension of asset management. Ceres Enterprises requires three bids from sourcing for items to be purchased to ensure the best products at the best price. Timely purchasing and detailed scheduling of deliveries are critical to the operation of our hotels. This also includes purchasing of items for renovations, the interviewing of installers and general contractors. Complete coordination ensures a smooth process with the minimal interference with hotel business along with the most economical and timely completion of all projects.
